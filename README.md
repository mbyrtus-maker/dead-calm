# Dead Calm

A first-person 3D murder mystery aboard a small cruise steamer. Every case is freshly generated: a new victim, killer, weapon and set of alibis. Search three decks, question the suspects as they wander the ship, cross-check their statements in the casebook, and make an arrest before dawn. Arrest the wrong person, or run out of time, and you're next.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## Controls
- Click the view to look around with the mouse; WASD to walk, Shift to hurry
- E to talk to someone or examine an item, F to search a room
- Walk the Grand Staircase to change decks (or press E at its foot)
- Click the deck plan in the corner to walk somewhere automatically
- On phones: left thumb to walk, swipe the right side to look, tap the round button to act

Co-op play only works when the game is opened as a Claude artifact; on GitHub Pages it plays solo.
